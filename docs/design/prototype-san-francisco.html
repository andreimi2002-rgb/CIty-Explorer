San Francisco

California, United States

A consolidated city and county on the tip of a peninsula, with ocean on one side and the bay on the other. Most of its official area is water: 121.5 km² of land inside 600.6 km².

Golden Gate Bridge

Opened 27 May 1937. Links the city to Marin County across the strait.

Fog and cool summers

September is the warmest month (average high 21.2 °C); July averages 19.1 °C.

Dense on a small footprint

873,965 people in 2020 on 121.5 km² of land: about 7,190 per km².

Founded in 1776

The Presidio and Mission Dolores were established by Spanish settlers in 1776.

Data & sources

Where every number on this page comes from, and what still needs checking.

  • U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts: San Francisco city, plus Los Angeles, San Diego, San Jose, Oakland. Public domain (U.S. government work).
  • Wikipedia: San Francisco for area, elevation, history and the NOAA climate table. CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Wikipedia: BART and Wikipedia: Muni for lines, stations and ridership.
  • To check: Mount Davidson is 283 m in the article text and 285 m in its infobox. The page uses 283 m until the USGS value is confirmed.
  • To check: the monthly precipitation values add up to about 582 mm, while the article text quotes 601 mm a year. In the app, normals come straight from NOAA's 1991–2020 dataset.
  • Not in this prototype: photos (the published page cannot load outside images, so the pictures here are drawings), live weather, metro-area population and neighborhood boundaries.
